Breaking Bad

---
Quick Facts
- Genre: Crime drama
- Seasons: 5
- Creator: Vince Gilligan
- Network: AMC

Why I Love It

1. The writing is tight, and early details pay off seasons later.
2. The character development is some of the best on TV.
3. The cinematography makes even a desert look tense.

Main Characters

| Jesse Pinkman  | Aaron Paul     | Former student        |
| Skyler White   | Anna Gunn      | Walter's wife         |
